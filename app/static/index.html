Scientific Scope & Limitations

  • Proxy vs. Wet-Lab Sensory Data: Basic taste vectors are reference-calibrated curated proxies, not electronic tongue or human sensory panel measurements. Human-perceived recipe taste remains unvalidated without sensory panel ground truth.
  • Ingredient Presence vs. Quantity: Public recipe corpora lack gram weights; unweighted averages equate trace spices with bulk staples.
  • Evenness vs. Culinary Balance: Shannon evenness J quantifies uniformity of the 5 modeled taste shares, not hedonic palatability.
  • Chemesthesis & Aroma Separation: Capsaicin heat, piperine, gingerol, and volatile FlavorDB odors are explicitly separated from the 5 basic lingual taste modalities.
