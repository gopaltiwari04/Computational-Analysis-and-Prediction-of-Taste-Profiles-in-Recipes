## 3. Remaining Verified Limitations

1. **Absence of Recipe-Level Human Sensory Ground Truth:** Public recipe datasets (`train.json`) do not include human sensory panel taste ratings; supervised ML metrics evaluate recovery of or co-occurrence around a lexicon-derived proxy label (`dominant_taste`), not human taste perception.
2. **Absence of Measured Ingredient Quantities:** Unweighted presence averaging assigns equal contribution to trace seasonings and bulk staples; `agg_weights` support exists in code but remains unused until a gram-annotated corpus is supplied.
3. **Unmatched Long-Tail Ingredients & Cooking Chemistry:** Of 6,195 unique normalized ingredients in the Kaggle corpus, 6,073 long-tail or composite items (e.g., `chili powder`, `garam masala`, `salsa`, `tortillas`) remain unmatched and are logged explicitly rather than assigned speculative taste scores. Thermal reactions (Maillard browning, caramelization, dilution) are omitted.
4. **Raw Data Redistribution Restriction:** `data/raw/train.json` is excluded from Git and Docker builds because the Kaggle / Yummly playground dataset carries no open-source redistribution license.
